01 // ENTER THE ARENA

More than a bracket.
It’s a community moment.

The Duffine Tourneys are special livestream events created by Duffball16 to celebrate channel milestones with the people who made them possible.

Duffinians get the chance to join the action, compete, laugh, and create the sort of unpredictable highlights that only happen live. Rocket-powered car football is a major part of the fun, while the exact game mode and event format can change from one Tourney to the next.

02 // MATCH FLOW

How a Duffine Tourney works

The format may change per event. The route into the arena stays simple.

  1. 1
    GET THE SIGNAL

    Follow the announcement

    Dates, game mode, eligibility, and lobby details are shared before each event.

  2. 2
    CLAIM YOUR SPOT

    Register through the community

    Join the Discord, read the event post, and follow the sign-up instructions or join live on YouTube and follow the instructions in the stream chat.

  3. 3
    BE MATCH-READY

    Check in before kick-off

    Have the game updated, your username ready, and arrive before the stated check-in time.

  4. 4
    LIGHTS ON

    Play live with Duffball16

    Join the lobby, respect the rules, and enjoy the chaos—win or lose.

06 // QUICK ANSWERS

Before you
queue up.

What is a Duffine Tourney?+

A creator-led community competition hosted by Duffball16, usually as part of a channel celebration or special livestream. The goal is competitive fun and community involvement.

Is every Tourney Rocket League?+

Rocket-powered car football is strongly associated with Duffball16’s content, but the exact game and mode should always be confirmed in the event announcement.

How do I enter?+

Join the official Discord and follow the registration steps in the next Tourney post Or join live on YouTube and follow the instructions in the stream chat. Spaces may be limited, so watch announcements closely.

Do I need a specific rank or platform?+

Requirements can vary between events. Any rank, platform, region, age, or team-size rules will be listed with the individual Tourney.

Where can I watch?+

Event streams and previous community content can be found on the official Duffball16 YouTube channel.
