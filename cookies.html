What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They can help websites remember information about a visit, such as preferences or other browser-related information.

How We Use Cookies

  • Essential cookies: These may be needed for parts of a website to function correctly.
  • Analytics technologies: Where used, these can help understand how visitors interact with a website and where usability can be improved.
  • Advertising technologies: Google advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies when advertising is enabled on the site.

Your Choices

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some technologies may affect how certain website features work.

For more information about Google's advertising technologies, visit Google's advertising technology information.
