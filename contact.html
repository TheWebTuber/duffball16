02 // COLLABORATIONS

SEND A BETTER COLLAB REQUEST.

You do not need a perfectly finished plan before reaching out. A useful request simply explains who you are, what you want to make, how it would be recorded, and when you are normally available.

WHAT HELPS MOST

Give enough detail to start a real conversation.

A message such as “wanna collab?” may be friendly, but it does not explain what you are proposing. You do not need to fill in every field below, but these details make it much easier to understand the idea and check whether it could work.

01
Who you are

Your name or creator name, platform, channel link and the type of content you make.

02
The actual idea

Explain the game, video, stream, D&D session, event or other project you want to make.

03
Recording & people

Say whether it will be streamed or recorded, where it may be posted and who else is involved.

04
Timezone & availability

Include your timezone and normal free windows, especially for recurring or multi-session projects.

05
Time commitment

Give a rough session length and say whether the idea is one-time, weekly, bi-weekly or flexible.

READY TO COPY

Collaboration Email Template

This is only an example to make reaching out easier. Copy it, paste it into your own email, replace the [bracketed text], remove anything you do not need, and make it your own.

TOduffball16@gmail.com
SUBJECTCollaboration Request – [Your Name / Channel]

Hi Duffball16,

My name is [your name / online name], and I create content as [your channel or creator name] on [YouTube / Twitch / TikTok / other]. I wanted to reach out because I have a collaboration idea that I think could be fun.

The idea is [briefly explain what you would like to do]. I normally create [type of content], and you can find my channel here: [channel link].

The collaboration would be [recorded / livestreamed / both] and would probably take around [estimated time]. If other people are involved, they would be [names / channels / still being arranged].

My timezone is [timezone]. I am normally most available [days and approximate times]. If this would be a recurring project, I could usually do [weekly / once every two weeks / monthly / flexible].

If the idea requires a specific game, server, software, mods, payment, preparation, or anything else important, the details are: [add anything relevant here].

There is no pressure if the idea does not fit your schedule or content. I mainly wanted to share it and see whether you might be interested. I am also happy to discuss or adjust the idea together.

Thanks for taking the time to read this.

Kind regards,
[your name / online name]
[channel name]
[channel link / contact details]

i

You do not have to keep every paragraph. The best email is one that gives enough information to understand the idea without making the message unnecessarily long.

03 // WEBSITE HELP

MAKE YOUR MESSAGE EASIER TO CHECK.

If you are reporting a website problem, including a few details can make the issue much easier to understand.

01
Tell us which page

Include the page name or URL where you noticed the problem.

02
Explain what happened

Say what you expected to happen and what happened instead.

03
Add useful device details

If relevant, mention whether you were using a phone or computer and which browser you used.

04
A screenshot can help

If the problem is visual, a screenshot may make it much easier to identify.

04 // PRIVACY

BE CAREFUL WHAT YOU SEND.

Email is useful for questions and support, but avoid sending passwords, payment-card details, account passwords, or other information that is not needed for your request.
